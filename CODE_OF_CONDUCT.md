# Code of Conduct

We want Pulse to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and contribute without being harassed or put down.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including towards people new to the project.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers decide what goes into the project, and say so politely when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.

## Scope

This applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, email **ink@invisibleloop.com**. Reports are handled privately.
